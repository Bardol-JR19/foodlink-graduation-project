# FoodLink – A Digital Surplus Food Redistribution Platform

## Project Description
Food waste and food insecurity are connected problems. Restaurants and supermarkets discard safe food while charities struggle to feed people in need. FoodLink connects food businesses with nearby charities to redistribute surplus food.
## Objective
To design and develop a platform where businesses can publish food donations and charities can search, reserve, and collect them.

## Target Users
- **Food Donors:** Commercial managers posting leftover food items (quantity, pickup window, address).
- **Charities:** Verified representatives searching, reserving, and collecting food donations.
- **Administrator:** System manager handling account verification and platform statistics.

## Scope & Requirements Summary (Week 2)
- **In Scope:** User accounts with roles, donation posting, search, and reservation.
- **Out of Scope:** Online payments, route optimization, AI recommendations, and native mobile apps.
- **Key Functional Requirements:**
  - FR-01: User registration and login (Donor, Charity, Admin).
  - FR-02: Donors can create food donations.
  - FR-03: Charities can view and filter available donations.
  - FR-04: Charities can reserve donations and get a pickup code.
- **Key Non-Functional Requirements:** 
  - Performance (load list under 3 seconds), Validation (reject empty/past dates), Security (hashed passwords and access control).
